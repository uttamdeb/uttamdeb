## Hi, I'm Uttam 👋

I build the analytics and AI systems behind **[10 Minute School](https://10minuteschool.com)** — Bangladesh's largest edtech, serving **2M+ monthly learners**.

- 🔭 Building the intelligence layer of **[TenTen](https://uttamdeb.com/tenten)** — RAG retrieval, semantic search, long-term memory & evaluation loops behind an AI learning assistant
- 🤖 Shipping internal agents at **[Origin Labs](https://uttamdeb.com/origin-labs)** — a Data Agent that turns plain questions into governed BigQuery SQL, and a QC Agent that made content QC **19–23× faster**
- 📊 5+ years of BI — BigQuery warehouse stewardship, Looker/Metabase dashboards, lead automation & ML lead scoring
- 👩‍🏫 Teaching **Python for AI/ML** at [SheSTEM](https://shestem.org/) — mentoring women entering STEM
- 🌱 Currently exploring local LLMs, fine-tuning & agent evals
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on AI agents, data tooling & edtech analytics
- 📫 Reach me: [uttamdeb670@gmail.com](mailto:uttamdeb670@gmail.com) · [uttamdeb.com](https://uttamdeb.com)
